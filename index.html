How nightly allocation works

When a guest pays in full on check-in, that payment is allocated against each night of their stay automatically:

Guest pays K700 on arrival
Room rate: K175/night
Total stay: 5 nights (K875)
─────────────────────
Night 1 → K175 allocated ✓
Night 2 → K175 allocated ✓
Night 3 → K175 allocated ✓
Night 4 → K175 allocated ✓
Night 5 → K175 owed — K0 remaining
─────────────────────
Balance due: K175

Billback / OTA guests (Expedia, Booking.com, company accounts) are flagged separately and excluded from the chase list.
